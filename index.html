This is a JavaScript implementation of the game Fringer, which was designed by Alexey Pajitnov and released in Microsoft Entertainment Pack: The Puzzle Collection in 1997.

Instructios

Use the left and right arrow keys or 'A' and 'D' to move the cursor and space, the up arrow, or 'W' to twist. On mobile, swipe left and right to move the cursor, and tap to twist.

Untie knots by matching the cursor's direction to the upper rope and then twisting. When a knot is fully untied, it disappears. Every few seconds, the knots will be pushed down and new knots will appear at the top. If a knot reaches the bottom, you lose.

Boosts allow you to switch the direction of the cursor. Untangle knots with sparkles to gain boosts. Gained boosts appear as dots along the sides of the board. Press tab, the down arrow, or 'S' to use boosts. On mobile, tap with two fingers to use boosts.

Press 'P' or the pause button to pause. On mobile, tap with three fingers to pause. Press 'N' or the replay button to start a new game. Change the start level using the dropdown.

Bonuses

The candle bonus begins to appear in level 2-2. Connect a rope between the top and bottom red indicators and that rope will disappear when the knots are next pushed down.

The paintbrush bonus begins to appear in level 3-1. When the paintrush appears onscreen, press enter or tap it to activate it. This will randomly recolor the ropes on the board and generate knew knots, which may or may not help you.

Toggle the star button to turn ond and off bonuses.
