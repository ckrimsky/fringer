This is a JavaScript implementation of the game Fringer, which was designed by Alexey Pajitnov and released in Microsoft Entertainment Pack: The Puzzle Collection in 1997.

Use the left and right arrow keys or 'A' and 'D' to move the cursor and space, the up arrow, or 'W' to twist. On mobile, swipe left and right to move the cursor, and tap to twist.

Untie knots by matching the cursor's direction to the upper rope and then twisting. When a knot is fully untied, it disappears. Every few seconds, the knots will be pushed down and new knots will appear at the top. If any knots reach the bottom, you lose.

Boosts allow you to switch the direction of the cursor. Untangle knots with sparkles to gain boosts. Gained boosts appear as dots along the sides of the board. Press tab, the down arrow, or 'S' to use boosts. On mobile, tap with two fingers to use boosts.

Press 'P' to pause. On mobile, tap with three fingers to pause. Press 'N' at any time to start a new game, or enter a level number at any time to start that level.
